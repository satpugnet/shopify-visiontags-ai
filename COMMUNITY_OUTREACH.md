## 2. Populate metafields on product creation thread

**URL:** https://community.shopify.com/t/on-product-creation-populate-metafields-with-pre-defined-data/334809
**Status:** [ ] Not posted

**Reply:**

This is still a pain point for a lot of merchants. Flow's metaobject support has been slow to land.

One alternative approach: rather than maintaining metaobject entries for every product type, you can use AI to analyze your product images and auto-generate metafield values, tags, descriptions, and SEO. For example, a "Ceramic Mug" image would get key features, materials, and dimensions filled in without needing predefined rules per product type.

I built [VisionTags](https://apps.shopify.com/visiontags-ai) to do exactly this. It looks at the product image and fills in tags, metafields, product descriptions, and SEO automatically. Works especially well when you have many product types and maintaining rules for each one is not realistic.
